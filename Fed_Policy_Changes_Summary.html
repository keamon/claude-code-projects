Policy Lessons Learned

Policy Lessons Learned

What Worked Well

✅ Aggressive Early Response

Swift action prevented financial system collapse
Coordinated fiscal-monetary policy
Market functioning preserved

✅ Clear Communication

Forward guidance anchored expectations
Transparent policy framework
Market preparation for policy shifts

✅ Flexible Framework

Average inflation targeting worked
Data-dependent approach
Willingness to adjust course

Areas for Improvement

Inflation Forecasting: "Transitory" assessment proved incorrect, delayed policy response by 6-9 months
Asset Bubble Risks: Ultra-low rates contributed to asset price inflation and risk-taking
Financial Stability: Need better integration of monetary policy and financial stability concerns

Framework Evolution

Enhanced inflation forecasting models
Greater focus on financial stability risks
Improved coordination with fiscal policy
Climate risk integration considerations
